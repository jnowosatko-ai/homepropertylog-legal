Home & Property Log

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Home Support

Home & Property Log is provided by Jerome Nowosatko, an individual. This policy describes how the iPhone app handles information. It is not legal, insurance, or claims advice.

What the app is

The app is a single-household organizational notebook: policy snapshot, rooms and items, photos, receipts, and an annual review. It does not file a claim, determine coverage, or require a Home & Property Log account.

Information stored on your iPhone

Records you type or attach are stored on your device in the app’s local store. If you delete the app, on-device data is deleted with it. Any iCloud copy remains under your Apple ID until you remove it.

iCloud / CloudKit

If iCloud is enabled and the CloudKit container is available, household records may sync through Apple’s private CloudKit database for this app. The developer does not operate a Home & Property Log server or account system. Apple processes iCloud data under Apple’s privacy practices.

If iCloud is off or the container cannot be opened, the app stays on the named on-device store.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to photograph or attach stills of rooms, items, receipts, or property conditions. The app can save an exported packet image to your library only if you ask it to.

Stills are stored as compressed JPEG in your household log. The app does not import video files (video is a path or link you type).

Purchases

State packs are one-time In-App Purchases processed by Apple. Apple handles payment, receipt, and restore. This app does not collect your payment card number.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

Home & Property Log does not include analytics SDKs, advertising, or tracking.

Contact

Use the support page. Once a public legal repository is published, you may also open a public GitHub Issue there. Do not send information you do not want posted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
