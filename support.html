What Home & Property Log is

A single-household organizational notebook for iPhone. It is not legal advice, insurance advice, claims advice, or an insurance product.

How to get help

After this site is published on a public GitHub repository with Issues enabled, open a public issue on that repository. Include what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. Issues are public — do not post policy numbers, claim numbers, or photos you do not want public.

What the free app includes

  • Welcome disclaimer (acknowledge to continue)
  • Policy snapshot, rooms and items, high-value, serial and equipment
  • Photo and video index (camera and library; video is a link or path only)
  • Receipts and a coverage-gap rollup labeled “your numbers, not a coverage determination”
  • Annual review and share-sheet export of PDF + CSV

Root tabs are Home · Log · Options · More. State packs are listed on Options, not as their own tabs.

In-app purchases

National inventory stays free. Each state pack is a separate one-time $4.99 unlock through Apple.

  • Colorado Front Range pack — product id com.jnowosatko-ai.HomePropertyLog.coloradoUnlock. Home Ignition Zone task log, storm and claim diary, winterize/freeze log, email of PDF + CSV to an adjuster.
  • California Wildfire Prep Pack — product id com.jnowosatko-ai.HomePropertyLog.californiaUnlock. Defensible space and home hardening log, CDI wildfire and claim diary, email of PDF + CSV to an adjuster.

Restore purchases from More → Unlock / restore after a reinstall. Use the same Apple ID.

What this app does not do

  • It does not file a claim or contact your insurer for you.
  • It does not replace a CAL FIRE inspection or local ordinance.
  • It does not guarantee a home will survive a wildfire or that a claim will be paid.
